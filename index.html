Test the planner’s accuracy

Example: the planner says you will have 12,500 after 1 year. A year later your account really holds 12,900. The error is +3.2%, which is inside ±5%.

  1. Set “How long you plan to invest” to a short period, such as 6 months or 1 year, then invest that way for real.
  2. Press “Save prediction”. It records today’s date and what the planner expects at the end.
  3. When the date arrives, come back, type your real balance, and press Check.
